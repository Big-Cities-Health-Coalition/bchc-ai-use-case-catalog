Before and after

Staff paste a notice, pick the audience, and get a plain-language draft plus a checklist of what must not change.

Original notice

Pursuant to the applicable state drinking water regulations, the Department hereby issues a precautionary boil water advisory for the affected service area following a loss of positive pressure in the distribution system. Customers are advised that water intended for consumption, food preparation, or oral hygiene should be brought to a rolling boil for a minimum period of one (1) minute prior to use, and that such advisory shall remain in effect pending receipt of two consecutive satisfactory bacteriological sample results.

Reading level: grade 16 · 1 sentence over 60 words

Plain-language draft

Boil your water before drinking it.

There was a drop in water pressure in your area, so your tap water may not be safe right now.

Boil water for at least 1 minute before you drink it, cook with it, or brush your teeth. Let it cool before you use it.

We are testing the water twice. We will tell you as soon as it is safe to drink again.

Reading level: grade 6 · longest sentence 19 words

Review checklist before this goes out

  • The required action is in the first line
  • Boil time and reason are unchanged from the original
  • No new instruction was invented
  • Legally required wording is preserved in the full notice
  • Translated versions reviewed by a bilingual staff member
  • Approved by the program lead

The kit drafts language. A communications staff member and the program lead approve every notice before it is published.
