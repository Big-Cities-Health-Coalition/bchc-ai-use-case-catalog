Home-visit note summarizer

A 20-minute typing task becomes a 4-minute review

Community health workers record a short voice memo after each home visit; the tool transcribes it, drafts the structured visit note in the case-management format, and queues it for the CHW to review and file.

AT A GLANCE

Home-visit note summarizer

What it does
Gives community health workers their afternoons back — the note is drafted before they reach the next driveway.
How it's built
On-device transcription, then an LLM maps the transcript onto the standard visit-note fields. The CHW edits and files; nothing files itself.
What it took
Pilot stage: two CHW teams, under $25k, one developer alongside the CHW supervisor.
Data & governance
Voice memos are deleted after transcription; drafts live inside the existing case-management system's access controls.
Reuse it
The prompt templates and the deletion schedule are documented in the entry for other departments to adapt.
