Usage: {% include resource-item.html item=item %} An off-site link on a host that site.yml `link_access` has a rule for shows the rule's name and access chip under its title, and the level's note and "Request access" link under the row; `access:` on the item overrides its host rule (link-meta.html). With no rule and no `access:` the row is exactly the markup resources/index.md carried before this include existed. Local assigns are prefixed `ri_` so nothing leaks into the caller's scope. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign ri_item = include.item -%}
